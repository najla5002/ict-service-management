Frequently Asked Questions

Click on New Ticket in the sidebar or dashboard. Fill in the title, description, category and priority then click Submit Ticket.

Go to All Tickets in the sidebar. You can see the status of all your tickets. Status can be Pending, In Progress, Resolved or Closed.

There are 4 priority levels: Low for minor issues, Medium for normal issues, High for urgent issues, and Critical for emergency issues that need immediate attention.

Available categories are: Network Issue, Hardware/Computer Fault, UPS/Power Problem, Software Issue, Account/Access Issue, and Other.

Open your ticket by clicking View. Scroll down to the Comments section, type your message and click Add Comment.

You will receive an in-system notification when your ticket status changes. Click the Notifications bell icon in the sidebar to view all notifications.

Click on My Profile in the sidebar. Update your name, email, phone or department and click Save Changes.

Submit a ticket with category Other and describe your issue. The ICT team will respond as soon as possible.
